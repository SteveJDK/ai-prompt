Competitive Analysis Report

📁 Business 🤖 GPT-4 / Claude 📊 Advanced 📅 Apr 26, 2026
competitive-analysis market-research strategy

Generate a detailed competitive analysis with strengths, weaknesses, and positioning.

📋 Prompt

You are a market research analyst. Create a competitive analysis for:

**Market/Industry:** [DESCRIBE]
**My Product:** [DESCRIBE]
**Competitors:** [LIST 3-5 COMPETITORS]

Provide:
1. **Competitor Profiles** - For each: product, pricing, target market, strengths, weaknesses
2. **Feature Comparison Matrix** - Table comparing features across all competitors
3. **Pricing Analysis** - How prices compare and where you fit
4. **SWOT Analysis** - For your product relative to competitors
5. **Market Gaps** - Underserved needs none of the competitors address
6. **Positioning Strategy** - How to differentiate and win
7. **Action Items** - 5 immediate actions based on this analysis

Be brutally honest about weaknesses and threats.

💡 Tips

  • List 3-5 direct competitors
  • Specify your market segment

Professional Email Reply

📁 Email 🤖 All Models 📊 Beginner 📅 May 5, 2026
email professional communication

Draft professional email replies for any business situation.

📋 Prompt

You are a professional communication expert. Draft a reply to:

**Original Email:**
[PASTE THE EMAIL YOU RECEIVED]

**My Response Goals:**
[WHAT YOU WANT TO ACHIEVE WITH YOUR REPLY]

**Tone:** [FRIENDLY/FORMAL/URGENT/DIPLOMATIC/FIRM]

Provide:
1. **Reply Draft** - Professional, clear, and complete
2. **Subject Line** - If starting a new thread or need to change the subject
3. **Key Points Covered** - Checklist of what the reply addresses
4. **Follow-Up Plan** - When and how to follow up if no response

Keep it under 150 words. Use short paragraphs. One idea per paragraph.

💡 Tips

  • Paste the original email
  • Specify your desired tone

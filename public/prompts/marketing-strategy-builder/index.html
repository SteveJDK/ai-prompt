Marketing Strategy Builder

📁 Marketing 🤖 GPT-4 / Claude 📊 Advanced 📅 Apr 11, 2026
strategy go-to-market planning

Build a complete go-to-market strategy with channels, messaging, and KPIs.

📋 Prompt

You are a CMO with experience at top tech companies. Build a go-to-market strategy for:

**Product:** [DESCRIBE]
**Stage:** [PRE-LAUNCH/LAUNCH/GROWTH]
**Budget:** [BOOTSTRAPPED/MID/HIGH]
**Target Market:** [DESCRIBE]

Provide:
1. **Positioning Statement** - One sentence that defines the product
2. **Target Personas** - 3 detailed buyer personas with pain points
3. **Channel Strategy** - Top 5 acquisition channels ranked by expected ROI
4. **Content Plan** - 30-day content calendar with topics per channel
5. **Messaging Matrix** - Key message for each persona per stage of the funnel
6. **KPIs** - 5 metrics to track with target values for first 90 days
7. **Budget Allocation** - Percentage breakdown across channels

Be specific and actionable. No generic advice.

💡 Tips

  • Define your product stage (pre-launch/launch/growth)
  • Specify budget range

Keyword Research Assistant

📁 Seo 🤖 All Models 📊 Beginner 📅 Apr 13, 2026
keyword-research search-volume content-ideas

Discover high-value keywords with search intent and content gap analysis.

📋 Prompt

You are an SEO keyword researcher. For the topic [YOUR TOPIC], provide:

1. **Primary Keywords** - 10 high-volume keywords (estimate search volume)
2. **Long-tail Keywords** - 20 specific queries with lower competition
3. **Question Keywords** - 10 how, what, why, best queries
4. **Search Intent Classification** - Informational, commercial, transactional, or navigational for each
5. **Content Gap Analysis** - Keywords competitors rank for that you don't
6. **Content Calendar** - 12 article ideas mapped to these keywords with priority ranking

Format as tables. Prioritize keywords by: (Search Volume / Competition Difficulty) ratio.

💡 Tips

  • Start with a broad topic
  • Specify your niche

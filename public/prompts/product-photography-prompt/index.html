Product Photography Prompt

📁 Image-Generation 🤖 Midjourney 📊 Intermediate 📅 Apr 15, 2026
midjourney product commercial

Create professional product photography prompts for e-commerce and marketing.

📋 Prompt

Generate a Midjourney prompt for professional product photography:

**Product:** [DESCRIBE PRODUCT]
**Style:** [MINIMAL/LUXURY/PLAYFUL/TECHNICAL/CORPORATE]
**Color Palette:** [COLORS]
**Use Case:** [WEBSITE/ADS/SOCIAL MEDIA/PACKAGING]

Create a prompt that includes:
- Professional product photography setup
- Specific lighting (softbox, rim light, etc.)
- Appropriate background and props
- Camera angle and composition
- Brand-appropriate color grading

Format: Professional product photography of [PRODUCT], [LIGHTING], [BACKGROUND], [COMPOSITION], [COLOR GRADING], commercial quality --v 6 --ar 4:5 --style raw

Provide 4 variations: clean white background, lifestyle context, hero shot, and detail/macro.

💡 Tips

  • Specify product type and brand aesthetic
  • Include color palette preferences

prompt_text: |
  You are an SEO specialist. Generate optimized meta tags for:

  **Page URL:** [URL OR DESCRIBE PAGE]
  **Target Keyword:** [PRIMARY KEYWORD]
  **Secondary Keywords:** [2-3 RELATED KEYWORDS]

  Provide:
  1. **Title Tag** - Under 60 chars, keyword at the beginning
  2. **Meta Description** - Under 160 chars, compelling with keyword
  3. **H1 Tag** - Different from title but includes keyword
  4. **URL Slug** - SEO-friendly URL structure
  5. **Open Graph Tags** - og:title, og:description, og:type
  6. **Twitter Card Tags** - twitter:title, twitter:description, twitter:card
  7. **Canonical URL** - Self-referencing canonical
  8. **Schema Markup** - JSON-LD for the appropriate schema type

  For each, explain why it is optimized.
tips:

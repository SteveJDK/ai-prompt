  You are a social media manager. Create a 30-day content calendar:

  **Platforms:** [TWITTER/LINKEDIN/INSTAGRAM/TIKTOK]
  **Niche:** [YOUR NICHE/INDUSTRY]
  **Content Pillars:** [3-5 TOPIC CATEGORIES]
  **Posting Frequency:** [POSTS PER DAY]
  **Goal:** [GROWTH/ENGAGEMENT/SALES/BRANDING]

  Provide a table with:
  - Day 1-30
  - Platform(s)
  - Post type (educational, entertaining, promotional, engagement, story)
  - Topic/headline
  - Content pillar
  - Format (text, image, video, carousel, thread)

  Rules:
  - 80 percent value, 20 percent promotion
  - Rotate through all content pillars evenly
  - Include engagement prompts at least 3x per week
  - Vary formats (do not post only text)
  - Include 2 viral attempt posts per week

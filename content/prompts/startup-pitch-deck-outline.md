prompt_text: |
  You are a startup advisor who has helped 50+ companies raise funding. Create a pitch deck outline:

  **Startup:** [DESCRIBE YOUR STARTUP]
  **Stage:** [SEED/SERIES A/SERIES B]
  **Funding Ask:** [AMOUNT AND USE OF FUNDS]
  **Key Metrics:** [REVENUE, USERS, GROWTH RATE, ETC.]

  Create a 12-slide pitch deck:
  1. **Title** - One-line description
  2. **Problem** - Pain point with data
  3. **Solution** - Your product
  4. **Market Size** - TAM, SAM, SOM
  5. **Product** - How it works (with demo screenshots placeholder)
  6. **Business Model** - How you make money
  7. **Traction** - Key metrics and milestones
  8. **Competition** - Competitive landscape and moat
  9. **Team** - Why you are the right team
  10. **Financials** - Projections for 3 years
  11. **Ask** - How much and what for
  12. **Vision** - Where this is going

  For each slide, provide the headline, 3-5 bullet points, and suggested visuals.
tips:
  - Specify your funding stage
  - Include your key metrics if available

  You are a study strategies expert. Create an exam preparation plan:

  **Exam:** [NAME AND TYPE OF EXAM]
  **Date:** [EXAM DATE]
  **Topics to Cover:** [LIST ALL TOPICS/CHAPTERS]
  **Current Knowledge:** [SELF-RATE EACH TOPIC 1-5]
  **Hours Available Per Day:** [NUMBER]

  Create a study plan using spaced repetition and active recall:
  1. **Schedule** - Day-by-day plan from now until exam date
  2. **Study Method** - For each topic: read, practice, review cycle
  3. **Practice Questions** - How many per topic and where to find them
  4. **Review Days** - Scheduled spaced repetition review sessions
  5. **Mock Exams** - When to take full practice tests
  6. **Final Week Plan** - Intensive review strategy for the last 7 days
  7. **Exam Day Strategy** - Time management and question approach

  Prioritize weak areas first. Use the 80/20 rule for maximum score improvement.

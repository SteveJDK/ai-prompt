prompt_text: |
  You are a professional blog writer. Write a comprehensive blog post on:

  **Topic:** [YOUR TOPIC]
  **Target Audience:** [DESCRIBE]
  **Target Keywords:** [KEYWORDS]
  **Word Count:** [1500/2000/3000]

  Structure:
  1. **Catchy Title** - Under 60 characters, includes main keyword
  2. **Hook Introduction** - Start with a surprising stat, question, or story
  3. **Body Sections** - 4-6 H2 sections with H3 subsections, each with actionable content
  4. **Conclusion** - Summary plus clear call-to-action
  5. **Meta Description** - Under 160 characters for SEO

  Rules:
  - Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
  - Include bullet points and numbered lists
  - Write in active voice
  - Use transition words between sections
  - Avoid filler words and fluff
tips:
  - Specify target audience
  - Include 3-5 target keywords

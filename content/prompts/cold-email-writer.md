description: "Write personalized cold emails that get responses, not deletions."
model: "All Models"
difficulty: "Beginner"
prompt_text: |
  You are a cold email expert. Write a cold email:

  **My Product/Service:** [DESCRIBE]
  **Recipient:** [TITLE, COMPANY, INDUSTRY]
  **Goal:** [BOOK A CALL/DEMO/MEETING]
  **My Credibility:** [SOCIAL PROOF, RESULTS, CLIENTS]

  Write 3 versions:

  **Version 1 - Short and Direct** (under 75 words)
  - One sentence about them
  - One sentence about what you offer
  - One sentence with a specific ask
  - No fluff, no introductions

  **Version 2 - Value-First** (under 120 words)
  - Lead with a specific insight about their company
  - Offer something valuable for free
  - Soft ask at the end

  **Version 3 - Case Study** (under 150 words)
  - Story of how you helped a similar company
  - Specific numbers and results
  - Ask if they want similar results

  Rules: No I hope this finds you well, no just checking in, no attachments, one clear CTA.
tips:
  - Describe your target recipient
  - Specify what you are offering

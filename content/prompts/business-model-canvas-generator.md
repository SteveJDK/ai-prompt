prompt_text: |
  You are a business strategy consultant. Create a Business Model Canvas for:

  **Business Idea:** [DESCRIBE]
  **Type:** [B2B/B2C/MARKETPLACE/SUBSCRIPTION/OTHER]
  **Stage:** [IDEA/MVP/ESTABLISHED]

  Fill in all 9 blocks:
  1. **Customer Segments** - Who are the target customers?
  2. **Value Propositions** - What unique value do you deliver?
  3. **Channels** - How do you reach customers?
  4. **Customer Relationships** - How do you acquire, retain, and grow customers?
  5. **Revenue Streams** - How do you make money? (pricing models)
  6. **Key Resources** - What assets do you need?
  7. **Key Activities** - What must you do to deliver value?
  8. **Key Partnerships** - Who are your essential partners?
  9. **Cost Structure** - What are the major costs?

  For each block, provide 3-5 specific items. Highlight the riskiest assumption.
tips:
  - Describe your business idea in 2-3 sentences
  - Specify if B2B or B2C

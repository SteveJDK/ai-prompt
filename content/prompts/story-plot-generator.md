prompt_text: |
  You are a master storyteller. Create a detailed story plot:

  **Genre:** [THRILLER/ROMANCE/SCI-FI/FANTASY/MYSTERY]
  **Setting:** [TIME PERIOD AND LOCATION]
  **Tone:** [DARK/LIGHT/SATIRICAL/SERIOUS]

  Provide:
  1. **Logline** - One sentence summary
  2. **Main Characters** - Protagonist, antagonist, and 2 supporting with motivations
  3. **Three-Act Structure** - Setup, confrontation, resolution with key plot points
  4. **Major Conflict** - Central tension and stakes
  5. **Character Arcs** - How the protagonist changes from beginning to end
  6. **Twist** - One unexpected revelation
  7. **Theme** - The underlying message

  Make it original and emotionally engaging.
tips:
  - Specify genre and tone
  - Mention if you want twist endings
